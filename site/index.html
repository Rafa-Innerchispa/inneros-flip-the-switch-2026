INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

How to verify InnerOS LiveOps

This page is publicly accessible and read-only. It does not impersonate a real operator or run commands on infrastructure.

01 · Inspect live statusReview Switch, Mattermost and Qwen service checks. A reachable port alone is not proof of an agent response.
02 · Review recorded eventsThe Operations and Evidence sections read actual archived results from authenticated Mattermost conversations.
03 · Follow the proofFind the command, agent response, correlation ID, PASS/FAIL result, latency and optional SHA-256 receipt in the repository or authorized operator records.
04 · Reproduce with accessAuthorized reviewers can sign in to the self-hosted Mattermost room, send !ping, !status and !incident, and observe replies. Approval commands are restricted to trusted operators.

Evidence provenance: /api/liveops/status reads local service checks; /api/liveops/evidence reads sanitized recorded command/response results. The incident triage test uses synthetic anomaly inputs but real Switch, Mattermost and local Qwen integration. Public visitors cannot actuate devices.
